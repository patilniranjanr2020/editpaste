About EditPaste

EditPaste is a fast, local browser image cropping utility designed for rapid screenshot and image editing.

  • Zero Uploads: Processing occurs 100% locally in your browser memory via the native Canvas and File APIs. No server, database, or analytics.
  • Clipboard History: Click Paste from Clipboard or press Win + V to pick an image, then Ctrl + V.
  • 10 Aspect Ratios: Standard fixed ratios (1:1, 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, etc.) or freeform freestyle resizing.
  • Keyboard Accessibility: Arrow keys to nudge crop pixels, +/- to zoom, R to rotate, Ctrl+S to export.
